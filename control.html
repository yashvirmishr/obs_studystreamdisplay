Multistream chat

Combined live chat

Enter the channel usernames only, without @ or URLs. Messages from both platforms appear in one feed with platform labels. You can enter either channel or both. Leave both blank to hide chat.

The combined feed uses Unified Chat Lite, a third-party, read-only service. It does not let you send messages or moderate from this overlay.

Automatic YouTube music display

Now playing

Install the companion browser extension to detect the active YouTube video automatically.

The extension reads the video title and channel from your YouTube tab and sends that metadata to this local overlay. It does not access your YouTube account, capture audio or control playback.
